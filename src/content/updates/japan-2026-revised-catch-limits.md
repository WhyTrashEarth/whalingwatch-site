## The source and its date

This note was published on November 23, 2026. It explains a Japanese Fisheries Agency instrument **revised September 18, 2026**, rather than reporting a new November decision.

For January 1–December 31, 2026, the instrument lists total allowable catches (TACs) of **56 sei, 153 Bryde’s, 145 minke and 58 fin whales**. These figures are limits, not the numbers actually caught. [Read the revised Japanese TAC instrument, pages 1–4](https://www.jfa.maff.go.jp/j/whale/attach/pdf/index-121.pdf).

## What the amendment changes

The September amendment transfers eight Bryde’s-whale allocations from the coastal category to the factory-ship category. The species TAC remains 153. The allocation categories and the total answer different questions. [Read the September 18 amendment comparison](https://www.jfa.maff.go.jp/j/whale/attach/pdf/index-123.pdf).

## Translation and catch figures remain separate checks

Older English initial-allocation material should not be substituted for the revised Japanese document. The displayed totals use the Japanese instrument; unresolved translation, regulatory-history and catch-reconciliation questions remain visible in the [research-status record](/research-status/#japan).

No reconciled 2026 actual-catch total is presented here. For the wider legal and geographic context, see the [Japan profile](/countries/japan/).
