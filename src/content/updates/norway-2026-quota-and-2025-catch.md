## The source and its date

This reference note was published on November 23, 2026. The underlying government announcement is dated **January 30, 2026**; it is not a new November quota decision.

Norway’s Ministry of Trade, Industry and Fisheries announced a **2026 quota of 1,641 minke whales**. The ministry attributes the increase to unused quota carried over from earlier years. A quota is an authorised limit, not the number of whales caught. [Read the government’s announcement](https://www.regjeringen.no/no/aktuelt/hogare-vagekvalkvote-i-2026/id3147611/).

## The reported catch belongs to 2025

The same announcement reports **429 whales caught by 10 vessels in 2025**. That is the preceding year’s catch. It must not be labelled as a 2026 result or compared as if both figures describe actual catches in the same season. [Source: the January 30 announcement](https://www.regjeringen.no/no/aktuelt/hogare-vagekvalkvote-i-2026/id3147611/).

## What remains open

A final 2026 catch return has not been reconciled for this note. The ministry’s assessments of sustainability and animal welfare are its stated positions, not conclusions established by these two numbers.
