Short introductions are available in [Icelandic](/is/), [Japanese](/ja/), [Norwegian Bokmål](/nb/) and [Brazilian Portuguese](/pt-br/). They describe the project and how to contribute. Most content remains in English; these introductions do not constitute reviewed translations of the full site.

The [country coverage register](/countries/history/#coverage) accounts for all 33 entities in the October 9 research archive without presenting them as a country count. The [historical source catalogue](/library/history/) exposes all 47 original records and four follow-up records, distinguishing inspected evidence from discovery leads.

The [research-status page](/research-status/) records the disposition of all 34 baseline questions. Global economic comparability, country completeness and many closure and worker outcomes remain open.

The [Iceland season note](/updates/iceland-2026-season-and-reform/) now states explicitly that the season figure is news reporting, not an independently verified official return. RÚV’s rendered English report uses 34 days; the archive’s 94-day wording is not adopted. The duration difference remains unresolved.

The revised Japanese 2026 TAC and September allocation change were inspected in the original documents. They establish dated limits, not actual catches. In the Brazilian record, the raw-material arithmetic discrepancy and the unresolved meal-sales denominator remain visible; no disputed total has been silently corrected.

The original research ZIP is preserved. No global monetary total, invented number, new whale-biology section or unapproved artwork is added.
