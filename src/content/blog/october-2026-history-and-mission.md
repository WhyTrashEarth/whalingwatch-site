The new [historical economics guide](/historical-economics/) explains products, markets and livelihoods, with carefully scoped Canadian, Brazilian and Norwegian examples. The [country-history inventory](/countries/history/) links documented episodes to their sources and provides a CSV of the published entries.

The inventory includes modern countries, a historical state and a shared maritime network. It is not a complete global census. National cessation, company closure, government policy and legal prohibition remain separate questions. Provisional catches, unread quota tables and unresolved monetary observations have not been promoted into accepted figures.

The [About page](/about/) now states the project’s enduring purpose: to preserve an accessible, independent record whether whaling continues, changes or ceases. Participation and corrections remain subject to attribution and verification.

Header links now visibly identify the current page or section, including for assistive technology. About routing was checked, and capitalized About URLs now redirect to the canonical lowercase page.

Icelandic and Japanese publication routes are prepared. No translated edition is published in this release: reviewed text and review records are still needed. Language links and search-engine alternates will appear only for published equivalents.
