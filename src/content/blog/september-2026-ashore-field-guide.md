Whaling Watch has published the first substantial field guide in its broader research program: **When a Whale Comes Ashore**.

### What was added

- A practical overview of the choices that can follow the death of a large stranded whale.
- Clear distinctions between decomposition-driven rupture, deliberate examination, and intentional explosive disposal.
- Sourced case studies from Taiwan, Massachusetts, Newfoundland and Labrador, Aotearoa New Zealand, and Oregon.
- Successful transport, scientific preservation, and cultural management alongside more sensational incidents.
- A visible research ledger naming disputed or incomplete material that has not yet been published as fact.

### Editorial decision

The 2013 Faroe Islands case and conflicting measurements for the 2004 Tainan sperm whale remain outside the factual narrative until stronger original documentation is secured. Sonar and live-stranding causation will be treated as a separate evidence review.
