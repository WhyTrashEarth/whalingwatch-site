The [source library](/sources/#historical-sources) now includes five historical additions as entries 14–18. The original thirteen entries retain their wording, links and earlier review date.

The additions connect Ulsan’s local documentation of Daegok-ri with UNESCO’s inscription record, a 2026 study of whale-bone harpoons from coastal Brazil, the Dutch National Library’s *Visboeck* record and BHL’s catalogue of William Scoresby’s 1820 account.

The supplied research notes are dated October 21; publication checks were carried out on October 24. The Brazilian study’s inference of likely hunting is preserved without a claim about the world’s first whalers. The wider Korean property’s chronology is not assigned to every carving. Historical descriptions are distinguished from modern scientific authority, and no images are reproduced.

The placeholder for an unverified public-submission record has been removed from Resources, together with its related incomplete-record notices. The historical country, economic and worker-outcome research questions remain unchanged.
